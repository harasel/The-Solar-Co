Solar performance

Solar panels still respond to available daylight, but output reduces in poor weather and during winter. The supplied guide notes that output may reduce by roughly 25–50% in these conditions; actual results depend on the system, location and weather.

Solar performance

Solar panels do not produce energy after sunset. If your system includes a battery, excess energy generated during the day can be stored for use at night. Otherwise, a grid-tied home draws the electricity it needs from the grid.

Storage & grid

A battery may be able to supply stored energy during an outage, but backup operation depends on the system selected. Storage and backup, sometimes called emergency power supply, are not automatically the same—this should be discussed during system design.

Storage & grid

A battery stores solar energy that is not used at the time it is generated. That energy can then be used later, such as in the evening, helping you use more of your own generation.

Storage & grid

A grid-tied solar home has a two-way connection. Your import/export meter records electricity drawn from the grid and excess solar sent back. Export arrangements and rates are set by your electricity retailer.

System life

The buyer’s guide explains that quality solar panels are designed for a service life of more than 25 years. Individual components have different expected lifespans, so product quality, correct installation and ongoing support all matter.

Before installing

Before installing

Ask whether they specialise in solar, how they assess energy use and roof conditions, who designs and installs the system, what monitoring is available, what product support applies and who helps after installation.

Before you choose

A thoughtful installer should ask questions too.

  • How and when do you use electricity?
  • What are your roof material, condition and dimensions?
  • Which roof faces receive the best sun?
  • Are trees, chimneys or buildings creating shade?
  • Are your energy needs likely to change?
  • Do you need storage, backup, or both?
