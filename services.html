Solar services

One energy goal. A system designed to meet it.

From rooftop generation to battery storage, every service connects back to the way your property uses energy.

01

Residential solar

Turn your roof into a source of everyday energy.

A home system is designed around roof conditions, household demand and when energy is used. Solar powers appliances first, with the grid supplying any additional electricity required.

  • Roof and shading considerations
  • Household energy profile
  • Future needs and storage options
02

Commercial solar

Match daytime demand with daytime generation.

Commercial properties often use significant energy while the sun is available. A carefully planned system can help a business use more locally generated electricity as it is produced.

  • Site and load review
  • Scalable system planning
  • Professional installation coordination
03

Solar + battery

Hold onto excess solar for later.

Battery storage captures energy not used during the day. That stored power can then support evening demand and, where a compatible backup system is designed, selected loads during an outage.

  • Storage sized to energy patterns
  • Clear distinction between storage and backup
  • Integrated solar and battery design
