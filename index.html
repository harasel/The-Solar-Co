Why solar?

Put the sun to work for your property.

Solar turns your roof into a local source of electricity. Your appliances use that energy first, reducing the amount you need to draw from the grid.

Add battery storage and surplus daytime energy can be held for later—helping you use more of what your panels generate.

How solar works

From sunlight to usable energy—in six clear steps.

A well-designed system moves energy through your property automatically, prioritising solar power before additional grid electricity.

  1. 01☀

    Sunlight

    Daylight provides the energy that begins the process.

  2. 02▰

    Solar panels

    Photovoltaic cells convert sunlight into DC electricity.

  3. 03↯

    Inverter

    The inverter changes DC into usable AC electricity.

  4. 04⌁

    Switchboard

    Power is directed safely to the circuits in your property.

  5. 05⇄

    Meter & grid

    Your meter records electricity imported from or exported to the grid.

  6. 06▤

    Battery

    Optional storage keeps excess solar energy ready for later use.

Use energy when it works for you.

Solar only

Generate. Use. Export.

Sun→Home→Grid

Generate. Store. Use later.

Sun→Battery→Home

Store excess daytime generation and draw on it when the sun goes down or your household demand increases.
